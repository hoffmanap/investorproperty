Tracking the Financialization of Housing in El Paso

This project serves as an analytical framework tracking structural changes within the residential housing market of El Paso, Texas. By synchronizing municipal data and market listings, this interface maps real-time trends where traditional owner-occupied homes are transitioned into centralized financial assets.

Why does this matter? As private equity investment, institutional syndicates, and short-term vacation rentals scale, local market metrics register shifts in neighborhood density, potential tenant displacement, and abnormal pricing pressure.

STR "Scattered Hotel" Economic Impact

Evaluating the financial footprint of decentralized vacation rentals.

What is the "Scattered Hotel" effect?

When investors acquire homes to use exclusively as Short-Term Rentals (STRs) on platforms like Airbnb, they create a network of "scattered hotels" across residential neighborhoods. Unlike traditional hotels, these properties often bypass municipal Hotel Occupancy Taxes (HOT). The simulator below takes current market data to calculate potential municipal tax revenue that is either uncollected or could be captured through enforcement.
